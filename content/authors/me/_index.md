I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at [Queen's University](https://www.queensu.ca/) in Canada, working on autonomous vehicles, state estimation, and multi-sensor navigation. I completed my Ph.D. at Queen's University under the supervision of Prof. [Sidney Givigi](http://www2.cs.queensu.ca/people/profile.php?fname=Sidney&lname=Givigi) and Prof. [Aboelmagd Noureldin](http://www2.cs.queensu.ca/people/profile.php?fname=Aboelmagd%20&lname=Noureldin). My research combines invariant Kalman filtering, GNSS, radar, and deep learning to build robust positioning systems for autonomous ground vehicles.

Before my doctorate, I spent over a decade in industry leading technology ventures in digital manufacturing and computer vision, including a public investment from FAPESP and roles at Siemens Industry Software. I enjoy bridging rigorous research with real-world engineering challenges.
